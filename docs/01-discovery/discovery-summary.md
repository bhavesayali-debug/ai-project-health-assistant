# Problem Discovery Summary

## Project

**AI Project Health Assistant**

## Target User

IT / Software Project Managers working in Agile or hybrid environments with multiple cross-functional teams and stakeholders.

---

## PM Goal

Maintain a reliable and current view of project health so that delivery concerns can be identified early, appropriate decisions can be made, and accurate status can be communicated to stakeholders and leadership.

---

## Current Situation

Project-health information is distributed across multiple tools and communication channels such as:

- Jira
- Confluence
- SharePoint
- ServiceNow
- Sprint updates
- Project plans
- Risk and issue registers
- Defect trackers
- Meeting notes
- Microsoft Teams
- Email
- Action and dependency trackers

No single source necessarily provides the complete picture of project health.

---

## Current PM Process

**Collect Information**

↓

**Review and Validate Updates**

↓

**Correlate Information Across Sources**

↓

**Identify Risks, Issues, Blockers and Dependencies**

↓

**Assess Milestone and Delivery Impact**

↓

**Determine Overall Project Health**

↓

**Consolidate Status Into Reports / Dashboards**

↓

**Communicate and Escalate**

---

## Primary Pain Points

### 1. Manual Data Collection

The PM spends significant time gathering information from multiple project sources.

### 2. Manual Consolidation

Information must be interpreted and combined before a meaningful project-health view can be created.

### 3. Fragmented Information

Important project signals may exist across different tools, meetings, documents, and conversations.

### 4. Stale or Conflicting Information

Different sources may contain outdated or inconsistent project updates.

### 5. Hidden Risks and Dependencies

Important delivery concerns may initially appear as weak signals within meeting notes, team discussions, or individual workstream updates.

---

## Business Impact

If an important project signal is missed or identified too late, it may result in:

- Incorrect project-health reporting
- Delayed escalation
- Missed milestones
- Risks becoming active issues
- Unmanaged dependencies
- Stakeholder surprises
- Decisions based on incomplete information

---

## Core Problem Statement

> **Project Managers need to continuously connect and interpret information from multiple project sources to understand the true health of a project and identify concerns early. The fragmented and continuously changing nature of project information makes this process time-consuming and increases the possibility that important risks, dependencies, inconsistencies, or emerging delivery concerns may be identified too late.**

---

## Opportunity

There is an opportunity to reduce the manual effort involved in gathering, correlating, and consolidating project information while helping Project Managers identify important project-health signals requiring attention.

At this stage, no specific technology solution has been assumed.

---

## Discovery Conclusion

The problem is not simply a lack of project information.

The challenge is turning **large amounts of distributed project information into a reliable and actionable understanding of project health.**
